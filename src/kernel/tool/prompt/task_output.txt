Retrieves a delegated background task's output, result payload, and next-action guidance.

This is the normal way to collect a finished task. Prefer it over `task_get` when you want
what the task produced rather than its bookkeeping.

Parameters:
- task_id (required): from the launch call or `task_list`.

If the task is still running you get its partial output — that is a signal to wait, not a
reason to launch a second copy of the same work.
